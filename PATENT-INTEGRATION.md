# Proposed CityUHK Patent Integration

## Selected patent

- **Title:** Method and Apparatus for Matching Buyers with Sellers in a Marketplace to Facilitate Trade
- **CityUHK IDF:** 414
- **US patent:** US 11,030,690 B2, issued 8 June 2021
- **Inventors:** Liang Zheng and Chee Wei Tan
- **Applicant and assignee shown on the patent:** City University of Hong Kong
- **CityUHK KTO opportunity sheet:** TRL 3; the sheet labels the IP status as patent filed

## Why it is relevant

The patent describes a computerized secondary marketplace that receives buyer and seller bids, derives buyer and seller utility, and uses a matching matrix to facilitate trades. Its worked embodiment concerns mobile-data trading.

UniCycleHK proposes a concept-level adaptation for second-hand student goods:

| Patent concept | UniCycleHK concept adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer bid | Budget, desired category, university and handover preference |
| Seller bid | Asking price, condition, urgency and available handover point |
| Buyer-seller matching | Rank compatible local listings for each buyer |
| Matching matrix | Score candidate buyer-listing combinations |
| Administration revenue | Optional transaction commission considered by the business model |
| Bid revenue / utility balance | Balance successful handovers, buyer value and platform sustainability |

The prototype’s **Smart Match** button is only a transparent mock ranking. It scores local convenience, demonstrated category preference, recency, affordability and seller rating. It is not presented as the patented algorithm itself.

## Safe wording for the pitch deck

> **Proposed CityUHK IP integration:** UniCycleHK will explore adapting CityUHK’s buyer-seller marketplace matching technology (IDF 414; US 11,030,690 B2) to rank second-hand listings using student budget, category, campus and handover preferences. The current prototype demonstrates the user experience only. Licensing scope and technical implementation remain subject to confirmation with CityUHK KTO.

## Safe wording for the business plan

UniCycleHK proposes to explore CityUHK’s “Method and Apparatus for Matching Buyers with Sellers in a Marketplace to Facilitate Trade” as the technical basis for a future Smart Match module. The patent’s disclosed embodiment matches buyers and sellers in a secondary market for mobile data through utility and revenue optimisation. UniCycleHK would investigate whether these matching principles can be licensed and adapted to second-hand goods by replacing mobile-data bid parameters with asking price, buyer budget, product category, university, handover location and timing constraints. The current front-end uses a simplified mock score and does not constitute a licensed or claim-complete implementation.

## Verification and licensing boundary

- Do not claim that the team owns or has licensed the patent.
- Do not describe the current mock ranking as the patented algorithm.
- Do not call the patent an AI image-recognition technology.
- Confirm licensing scope, territory and adaptation feasibility with CityUHK Knowledge Transfer Office before commercial use.
